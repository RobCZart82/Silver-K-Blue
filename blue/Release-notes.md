Silver-K Blue 1.0.1 adds component installers and improves Windows VST3 offline rendering.

- The Windows x64 EXE offers VST3 and Standalone as selectable components.
- The macOS Universal 2 PKG offers AU, VST3 and Standalone for Apple Silicon and Intel.
- Portable ZIPs remain available for both platforms. The release includes source and SHA-256 checksums.
- Blue Standard and Retina 4K are built in, and Legacy remains available in Setup. Supply your own compatible firmware and factory data.

The Windows EXE and macOS PKG are unsigned. The macOS app and plug-in bundles are ad hoc signed. The macOS package is not notarized.

See the [installation guide](https://github.com/RobCZart82/Silver-K-Blue/blob/main/doc/INSTALL.md) for setup.
