# Silver-K 0.2.4 Alpha 1

The bank selector now lists populated folders, files containing multiple programs, and factory/storage banks. Individual preset files remain in the patch table and no longer fill the bank menu. The import tree is inspected on its loader thread and the menu updates asynchronously. Selecting another bank supersedes older menu requests. Rescan preserves the selected collection by its identity, even when its internal source nodes are recreated; Rescan All still includes individually imported files.

V-Station playback in the K engine reserves 6 dB through the firmware program-level parameter. This is an intentional reduction in playback level: loud polyphonic presets were reaching the K output ceiling. Original filter, overdrive, EQ and effect settings remain intact, and original source/library data are unchanged. The gain adjustment is applied on selecting a V-Station preset, including legacy V-tagged imports. It is not applied again when restoring the already adjusted saved engine state. Previously saved projects retain their saved level until the V-Station preset is selected again. KS playback and native K/A presets keep their previous level. No output limiter, added latency or callback processing was introduced.

Local macOS arm64 checks:

- Four scoped CTest checks passed: conversion, host clock, patch workflow and KS part MIDI.
- The supplied TranceStorms collection retained all 250 FXP rows in both engines, with its V-Station filter and click loading. The menu contained fewer than ten collections instead of hundreds of preset files. Refreshing the source preserved its selection and all rows; no FXP filenames returned to the menu.
- Presets from 13 name-prefix groups produced finite audible sound, with zero counted C++ allocations in the checked callbacks. K capture peak was 0.976; KS capture peak was 0.203. These short captures are not a universal guarantee for every performance or downstream effect chain.
- LD Bells Of Heaven, at 48 kHz / 256-frame blocks, MIDI velocity 127, volume 127, and the held notes C3/G3/C4/G4: the original K-level probe reached approximately 0.991 peak / 0.37 RMS; adjusted playback reached 0.689 peak / 0.185 RMS. Exact peaks vary with voice/effect phase. The original preset contains filter overdrive 61 and unison; these settings were retained. The separately tested KS probe reached 0.243 peak at volume 127 and did not exceed full scale.
- The second Bells file has the same internal name and uses all three oscillators. Its adjusted K chord probe reached 0.690 peak / 0.173 RMS, compared with approximately 0.991 peak before the adjustment.

The gain correction addresses K playback headroom. It does not remove intentional distortion from a preset or guarantee identical sound to the original V-Station software. Subsequent effects, mixer gain and audio-device settings can still affect the final level. Release archives include authored synthetic data and the current source snapshot; private presets and original firmware are excluded. macOS bundles are locally ad-hoc signed and are not notarized.
